Briefly between two lives.

An app for solo travelers at airports. One match, one true thing in common, one chance to step out from behind the screen — before either of you boards.

01a note from the makers

An airport is one of the rare places where everyone is between two lives.

Strangers, briefly equal, briefly stationary, all heading somewhere else. You have ninety minutes — a slow coffee, a book in your bag, three walks past the same five gates. Most of it ends up disappearing into a phone.

We thought there ought to be another option for the in-between.

So we made a small app. It does one thing. It finds one other person, in your terminal, who would also rather not spend the next hour staring at a screen — and it tells you one true thing the two of you have in common. You decide whether to meet, in person, before you board.

No feed. No swiping. No profile to scroll through. If neither of you wants to step out, nothing happens. The person on the other end never knows.

02how it works

One match. One reason. One terminal.

  1. You tap standby when you're at your gate. Nothing visible to anyone yet — just a quiet signal that you're open.

  2. We look for one other person, in your terminal, on standby too. Same airport, different destination. Always one match at a time.

  3. We surface a single specific reason to meet. Not a profile. Not a feed. One sentence — a fact you already share.

  4. A spot in the terminal. Two time windows. You either step out, or you don't. Then you board.
