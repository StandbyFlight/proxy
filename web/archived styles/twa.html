01
a note from the makers

An airport is one of the rare places where everyone is briefly between two lives.

Strangers, briefly equal, briefly stationary, all heading somewhere else. You have ninety minutes — a slow coffee, a book in your bag, three walks past the same five gates. Most of it disappears into a phone.

We thought there ought to be another option for the in-between. So we made a small app. It does one thing: it finds one other person in your terminal, surfaces a single true thing you share, and lets you choose whether to meet — in person — before either of you boards.

No feed. No swiping. No profile to scroll. If neither of you steps out, nothing happens. The other person never knows.

02
how it works · one match, one terminal
  1. Step 01
    Go on
    Standby.
    Tap once at your gate. Quiet signal. Nothing visible to anyone yet — just a note that you're open.
  2. Step 02
    One person
    found.
    In your terminal. Different destination. Same window. Always one match at a time — never a stack of cards.
  3. Step 03
    One true
    thing.
    We surface a single specific reason to meet. Not a profile. Not a feed. One sentence — a fact you already share.
  4. Step 04
    Step in.
    Or out.
    A spot in the terminal. Two time windows. You either step out and meet, or you don't. Then you board.
